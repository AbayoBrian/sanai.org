ABOUT US

Our Story

Sanai Holdings Ltd is a general merchandise supplies company. It was founded in 2014 and registered on 7th June 2014 under Companies Act. Its main focus is effective and efficient provision of general merchandise and supplies to both private and government sectors.

It pools together the efforts of young minds, with great interpersonal skills and versatility to perform duties. Impressive Solutions aims at provision of a wide variety of goods and services.

Our office located at Kenya House Complex, Koinange Street, Nairobi for effective management and coordination of all activities involved from sourcing, packaging, and delivery to monitoring the communication with the customers. Sanai Holdings Company Ltd believes that quality performance is a result of ability to understand client's specific needs, and provide goods and services to resolve the demands of the organization.

We at Sanai Holdings Company Ltd, will work with you by listening, understanding your needs and timely supply of the goods and services for effective sustainability of your company's / organization's operations. These efforts substantiate our pledge to perform all of our work in a safe manner and to meet and exceed the quality expectations of our customers.
